Good to know

Questions,
answered.

What material do you use?

We build with BWR (boiling-water-resistant) plywood as standard, finished in laminate, acrylic or veneer, with soft-close hinges and quality channels. Material choices are listed clearly in your quote.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. We visit your site in Hyderabad, take measurements, discuss designs and share a transparent, itemised quote at no cost.

Do you offer a warranty?

All our work is warranty-backed — up to 10 years depending on the material and hardware chosen. Your warranty terms are written into the quote.

Which areas do you serve?

We work across Hyderabad — including Serilingampally, Ameenpur, Miyapur, Chandanagar, Gachibowli, Kondapur and nearby areas.

Do you take small jobs and repairs?

Absolutely. From a single shelf or hinge repair to a complete 3BHK fit-out — no job is too small.
